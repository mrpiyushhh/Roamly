03 · Foundations

Typography

Space Grotesk for display, Inter for everything else — loaded from Google Fonts at 01:14 and wired both as a Tailwind key and a hand-written CSS class.

Space Grotesk

font-display
Ridge Run

Headings, prices, logo, stat numerals. Always tracking-tighter, usually uppercase, frequently with one italic word.

Inter

font-sans · default
Premium weekend trekking experiences for those who seek the silence of the summits.

Body copy, labels, badges, buttons, nav. Carries every micro-label at 9–11px with wide tracking.

Scale in use

Three rules carry the voice

Uppercase above body

Headings, buttons, labels, badges, nav, footer. Sentence case survives only in long prose — 01:92, 02:143.

Tracking inverts with size

Display type goes tracking-tighter (−0.05em); anything at or below 11px goes tracking-widest or [0.2em].

Italic as the accent mark

The last word of a headline flips to text-brand italic — 02:111, 04:69, 08:44, 09:74.

Issues

01

Two design languages inside one checkout

Critical

02

Every internal link is broken

Critical

Markup points at Home__desktop.html, BookingStep1__desktop.html and siblings. The files on disk are named 01-Roamly - Home.html. Nothing navigates as shipped.

03

Requested font weights are never loaded

Critical

The Google Fonts request loads Inter at 300;400;500;600, but the markup uses font-bold (700) and font-black (900) on nearly every label, button, and price. All of them clamp to 600. Space Grotesk stops at 700, so font-extrabold and font-black headings clamp too. The interface is measurably lighter than designed.

01:1405:14
04

bg-canvas referenced but undefined on four pages

Major

Bodies set class="bg-bg-canvas", but the token is absent from the compact configs. The class is a no-op; those pages render on the browser default rather than an explicit white.

04:31 vs 04:2007:31 vs 07:2008:31 vs 08:2005:26
05

Navigation by onclick, not anchors

Major

<button> and <div> elements push window.location.href. Not keyboard-reachable as links, no middle-click, no visible href, no browser affordances.

Major
