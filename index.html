Frequently Asked Questions

01 What fitness level is required for trips?
Each trail has a detailed grade from Easy to Extreme. Weekend Peaks suit beginner hikers with basic cardio, while Alpine Tours and Expert Ridges require prior endurance training and high-altitude experience.
02 What gear do I need to bring?
We provide all core technical gear (tents, high-altitude sleeping bags, crampons, satellite comms, and medical kits). You only need personal clothing, trekking boots, a daypack, and a reusable water bottle.
03 Can I join as a solo traveler?
Absolutely! Over 60% of Roamly climbers join solo. You'll be matched with a small cohort of up to 8 climbers led by professional certified mountain guides.
04 What is the cancellation and refund policy?
Full deposit refund up to 14 days before your trip start date. If weather forces a trail closure, we offer free rescheduling or 100% trip credit with no expiration.
